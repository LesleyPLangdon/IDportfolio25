Articulate Storyline Projects

These projects showcase my skills in Articulate Storyline 360, a powerful tool for creating engaging, interactive eLearning experiences. From quick, single‑scene interactions to complex, multi‑layered scenarios, each example demonstrates my ability to combine instructional design principles with Storyline’s triggers, layers, variables, and custom navigation. The collection highlights how I design learning experiences that are visually polished, intuitive to use, and built to keep learners actively engaged.

AI for Business

This project showcases a series of six micro-learning lessons designed to introduce learners to the practical applications, creative potential, and ethical considerations of artificial intelligence. Developed for diverse delivery formats, the project is tailored to engage learners with hands-on activities and collaborative tools.

Smart Goals

This course provides participants with the knowledge and skills necessary to effectively set and achieve personal and professional goals. Utilizing the renowned SMART framework (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-Bound), this interactive course will guide learners through the process of defining clear, concise, and actionable goals. Participants will learn to identify and overcome common obstacles to goal achievement, develop effective action plans, and track their progress toward success.
