Adaptive Assessments

These adaptive assessment templates, built in Articulate Storyline 360, are designed to deliver a personalized testing experience that adjusts to each learner’s demonstrated skill level. By dynamically modifying question difficulty in real time, they help keep learners challenged without overwhelming them, improve engagement, and provide richer insight into performance than fixed‑path quizzes.

I developed two distinct models:

  • Set‑Level Model — Adjusts difficulty after every set of three questions, making it ideal for assessments where performance is best measured in short, grouped segments.
  • Question‑Level Model — Adjusts difficulty after each individual question, enabling precise evaluation of a learner’s depth of knowledge for each concept.

Both templates are fully customizable, making them easy to adapt for different subjects, difficulty frameworks, and assessment needs. They demonstrate my skills in instructional design, branching logic, variable tracking, and custom player design while applying learning theories such as Bloom’s Taxonomy, mastery learning, and formative assessment principles.
