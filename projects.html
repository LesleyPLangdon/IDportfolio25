Instructional Design Projects

As an educator and instructional designer, I am passionate about creating innovative and engaging learning experiences that empower students to explore, create, and succeed. My projects blend modern tools, interactive technologies, and thoughtful andragogy to deliver lessons that resonate in today’s dynamic educational landscape.

Each project showcased here reflects not just the final product but the journey of thoughtful planning, design, and iteration that brought it to life. Click on any project to explore the tools, strategies, and methods I used to craft impactful learning experiences.

AI for Business

This project showcases a series of six micro-learning lessons designed to introduce students to the practical applications, creative potential, and ethical considerations of artificial intelligence. Developed for diverse delivery formats, the project is tailored to engage learners with hands-on activities and collaborative tools.
