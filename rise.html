Articulate Rise projects

AI for Business

Audience
Workplace learners who want to use AI tools for everyday business tasks.
Challenge
Introduce learners to the practical applications, creative potential, and ethical considerations of artificial intelligence, in a course that works across diverse delivery formats.
My solution

A series of micro‑learning lessons, each focused on one practical task:

  • What is AI, and What Can It Do for Me?
  • Polishing Communication with AI
  • AI for Meeting Preparation
  • Summarizing Long Documents with AI
  • Brainstorming and Creative Writing with AI
  • Creating Stunning Images with AI
  • Ethical and Responsible AI Use

By the end, participants can use AI tools to enhance communication, prepare meetings more efficiently, and summarize lengthy documents, explore AI’s creative potential, and understand the ethical considerations for responsible AI use in the workplace.

Key features / skills shown
  • Microlearning structure with hands‑on activities and collaborative tools
  • Screen‑recorded demonstrations of real AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, and OpenArt)
  • Ethics built in as its own lesson, not an afterthought
  • Responsive layout for desktop and mobile learners
