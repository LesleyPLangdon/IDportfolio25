About Me

I’m Lesley Langdon—a wife, mother, self-trained chef, weekend horticulturist, and lifelong learner pursuing an EdD in Curriculum & Instruction with a focus on Instructional Design & Technology. My professional journey has taken me from the fast-paced world of restaurant management to the dynamic challenges of real estate, and finally, to the rewarding field of education. Each step has shaped my ability to lead, adapt, and create meaningful experiences for those I serve.

I began my career in restaurant management, rising to the position of general manager at Chili’s, where I managed one of the company’s culinary test locations and a management training site. I gained hands-on experience in leadership, organizational strategy, and mentoring new managers—skills that directly influenced not only my team but also the broader network of restaurants we supported. While this career was rewarding, I knew it was just the beginning of my journey.

In real estate, I embraced the opportunity to connect with clients during some of the most significant moments of their lives. Whether helping buyers find their dream home or ensuring sellers received maximum value, I developed strong skills in negotiation, active listening, and customer-focused solutions. This career also introduced me to the evolving role of technology, sparking a passion for learning and applying tech tools that would later inspire my transition into computer science.

Balancing my career and family responsibilities, I pursued a degree in computer science while continuing to work in real estate. This period taught me the value of resilience, adaptability, and prioritization. As my daughter’s needs evolved, I chose to focus on my education full-time, ultimately earning a BS in computer science and beginning an MBA program.

In 2021, I embarked on a new chapter as a computer science educator, combining my technical knowledge and passion for helping others achieve their goals. Now, as I prepare to transition into instructional design, I bring with me a deep understanding of how to design learning experiences that are impactful, engaging, and adaptable to diverse audiences.

Thank you for visiting my site. Here, you’ll find examples of my work, insights into my mission, and ways to connect. I look forward to sharing my journey and continuing to help others succeed.
